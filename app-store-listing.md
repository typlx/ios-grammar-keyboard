## Description (4000 chars max)

Typist is an AI-powered keyboard that silently corrects your grammar as you type — in any app on your iPhone or iPad.

**How it works**

Type normally using the Typist keyboard. When you want to fix a sentence, tap the **Fix Grammar** button in the toolbar above the keyboard. Typist sends only that sentence to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) and shows you a corrected preview. Tap the button again to apply the fix, or ignore it and keep typing.

**Privacy first**

Your text is only sent to the AI provider when *you* tap "Fix Grammar". Typist never logs keystrokes, never records your text in the background, and never stores your words on any server. Your API key is stored securely in the iOS Keychain and never leaves your device.

**Bring your own AI**

Typist connects to the AI provider of your choice:

- **OpenAI** (GPT-4o mini, GPT-4o, or any OpenAI-compatible model)
- **Anthropic** (Claude Haiku, Claude Sonnet, or any Anthropic model)
- **Local / self-hosted** — coming soon for fully on-device correction with no cloud dependency

You supply your own API key. There are no hidden subscriptions, no Typist servers, and no per-correction fees beyond what your AI provider charges.

**Context-aware correction**

Tell Typist what you are writing so it can tailor corrections appropriately:

- **General** — everyday writing
- **Email** — formal and professional
- **Chat** — conversational and concise
- **Essay** — academic register
- **Code Comment** — precise, technical prose

**Getting started**

1. Open the Typist app and tap "Open Settings".
2. Go to **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard** and add **Typist Keyboard**.
3. Tap Typist Keyboard in the list and enable **Allow Full Access** (required for network requests).
4. Back in the Typist app, enter your API key in Settings.
5. Switch to the Typist keyboard in any text field and tap **Fix Grammar** anytime.

**System requirements**

- iOS 15 or later
- iPhone or iPad
- An active OpenAI or Anthropic API key
